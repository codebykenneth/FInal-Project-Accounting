Quick guide

  1. Journal - record each transaction: pick the debit account, the credit account and the amount. The same amount always goes on both sides.
  2. General ledger - choose an account to see its T-account and running balance.
  3. Worksheet - trial balance, adjustments, income statement, balance sheet and net income are worked out for you. Green means debit = credit.

Account types: Asset (what you own), Liability (what you owe), Capital (owner's investment), Drawing (owner takes money out), Revenue (money earned), Expense (cost of running). An adjusting entry is recorded at the end of the period: supplies used, insurance expired, depreciation.
